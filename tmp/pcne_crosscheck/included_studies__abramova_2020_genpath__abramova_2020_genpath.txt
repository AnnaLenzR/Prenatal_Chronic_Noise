Social and Cognitive Impairments in Rat Offspring
after Ultrasound-Induced Prenatal Stress
O. V. Abramova1, E. A. Zubkov1, Ya. A. Zorkina1, A. Yu. Morozova1,

Translated from Byulleten’ Eksperimental’noi Biologii i Meditsiny, Vol. 168, No. 12, pp. 687-691, December, 2019
Original article submitted August 27, 2019
We studied the possibility of developing an autism model based on chronic prenatal psycho-
logical stress caused by variable frequency ultrasound 20-45 kHz. The offspring of female
rats stressed during pregnancy demonstrated reduced time of social contacts in the social in-
teraction test, increased anxiety in the open-field test, and memory impairment in the Morris
water maze test in comparison with the control (intact) rat offspring. We also found a reducing
trend in the BDNF gene expression in the amygdala in males of the experimental group. The
results showed the possibility of developing the animal autism model based on prenatal stress.
Key Words: prenatal stress; ultrasound effect; autism spectrum disorder; autism model;
rat offspring
